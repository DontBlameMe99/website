## Why Would You Use a Split Keyboard?

A split keyboard offers several advantages over traditional keyboards, making it a popular choice for those seeking ergonomic benefits, customization options, and improved typing experience. I specifically chose it because of my already existing pain I had in my arms.

### Advantages of Using a Split Keyboard

Using a Split Keyboard has lots of advantages. Below you can find some of them.

#### Ergonomics

One of the primary reasons to use a split keyboard is its ergonomic design. The split layout allows you to position the two halves of the keyboard shoulder-width apart, reducing strain on your wrists, arms, and Shoulders. This positioning promotes a more natural and comfortable typing posture, potentially minimizing the risk of repetitive strain injuries ([RSI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Repetitive_strain_injury)) such as [carpal tunnel syndrome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carpal_tunnel_syndrome).

#### Customization

Split keyboards often come with programmable keys and layers, providing extensive customization options. You can remap keys, create macros, and configure layers to suit your specific needs and workflow. This level of customization allows you to optimize the keyboard layout according to your preferences, increasing Productivity and efficiency.

#### Ortholinear Lay Out

Unlike traditional keyboards with a staggered layout, split keyboards often feature an Ortholinear lay out. In an Ortholinear lay out, the keys are aligned in a grid pattern, reducing finger movement and making it easier to reach keys accurately. This arrangement can improve typing accuracy and reduce finger strain, especially when transitioning from a non-Ortholinear lay out.

#### Hot-Swappable Switches

Many split keyboards offer hot-swappable switch functionality, allowing you to change switches without soldering. This feature enables you to experiment with different switch types, such as linear, tactile, or clicky switches, to find the ones that best suit your typing preferences and needs. Switch swapping can be a straightforward process, providing flexibility and customization options.

#### Build Quality and Durability

Split keyboards are known for their high build quality and durability. They are often constructed using premium materials and components, ensuring longevity and withstanding heavy daily use. Investing in a well-built split keyboard can provide a reliable typing tool that will serve you for years to come.

#### Portability

The split design of keyboards offers an additional advantage in terms of portability. These keyboards can be easily disassembled into two smaller pieces, making them more convenient to carry in a bag or backpack while traveling. The compact size and ease of disassembly make them suitable for users who are frequently on the go.

#### QMK Firmware

Split keyboards often utilize the QMK firmware, an open-source keyboard firmware renowned for its versatility and customization options. QMK firmware allows you to program and customize your keyboard's behavior, including key mappings, layers, macros, and advanced features. This flexibility ensures compatibility with various operating systems and empowers users to tailor their typing experience to their liking.

## Learning and Adaptation

Learning to use a split keyboard can be a unique experience, especially if you are transitioning from a traditional keyboard or have never typed with ten fingers before. However, with some practice and dedication, most users can adapt to the split style relatively quickly.

### Learning Experience

Initially, using a split keyboard may feel unfamiliar and require an adjustment period. The split design and tilted halves might take some getting used to, as you learn to position your hands correctly and find a comfortable angle.

However, many users report that the split layout feels more natural and ergonomic once they adapt to it.

The challenge often lies in Learning to navigate the multiple layers and efficiently utilize the programmable keys. While the split keyboard offers customization options, setting up and memorizing the various layers and key mappings can take time and patience. It's essential to invest time in understanding the layers' organization and determining the most effective layout for your specific needs.

### Time Taken to Learn

The time required to learn the split style varies from person to person. For me it took around 3 to 6 hours to become comfortable with the split keyboard.

Notably, prior experience with touch typing can be advantageous, as it helps to adapt to the split layout more quickly.

It's important to note that the learning process doesn't end with basic proficiency. As you continue to use the split keyboard, you will likely discover new ways to optimize your workflow, customize your layout, and enhance your typing speed and accuracy.

## Recommendation

Whether a split keyboard is the right choice for you depends on various factors, including your individual needs, preferences, and willingness to adapt. Here are some considerations to help you make an informed decision:

If you experience discomfort or pain while typing on a traditional keyboard, a split keyboard's ergonomic design can provide relief and prevent potential injuries.

If you value customization and want to tailor your keyboard layout to match your workflow and programming needs, a split keyboard with programmable keys and layers offers extensive customization options.

If you desire improved typing accuracy and reduced finger movement, the Ortholinear lay out of split keyboards can enhance your typing experience.

If you enjoy experimenting with different switch types or want the freedom to swap switches easily, a split keyboard with hot-swappable switches allows for customization and personalization.

If you prioritize portability and need a keyboard that can be easily carried while traveling, the compact and disassemble design of split keyboards makes them a convenient choice.

Considering the learning curve and adaptation period, it's important to be prepared to invest time in familiarizing yourself with the split keyboard and its customization features. However, the potential benefits in terms of ergonomics, customization, and workflow optimization make it a worthwhile investment for many users.
