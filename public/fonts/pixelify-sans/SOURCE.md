# Pixelify Sans source

- Upstream: https://github.com/eifetx/Pixelify-Sans (SIL Open Font License 1.1;
  see OFL.txt, copied from https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/pixelifysans/OFL.txt).
- Input: the Google Fonts **latin** variable subset (`wght` 400–700), downloaded
  once and committed as-is. There is no local subset step.
- SHA-256: `4a5633a0c9c1b73abd133a56d3716c2d8df2ed03cb987346f72194aeb224f382`.

Coverage policy: Basic Latin, Latin-1 and the punctuation and symbols the site
copy uses. A character outside the subset falls back per glyph.
